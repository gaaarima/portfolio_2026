Rabina Beauty Parlor

Turning a word-of-mouth salon and training centre in Kathmandu into an 11-page website that turns visitors into booked appointments.

Role
Solo UX/UI & build
Timeline
About 3 weeks, 2026
Tools
HTML, CSS, vanilla JS
Scope
11 pages, responsive

Design thinking

  1. 1Empathize

    Founded by Sulochana, the salon grew one client at a time. New clients couldn't browse services or prices online.

  2. 2Define

    Two audiences, beauty clients and aspiring students, must understand, trust and act from a first visit on a phone.

  3. 3Ideate

    Lead with warmth, make services and prices scannable, and give both audiences a clear next step.

  4. 4Prototype

    A low-fidelity homepage locked the hierarchy before any colour or photo, then the full 11-page build.

  5. 5Test

    Checked from 375px phones to wide desktop, plus an accessibility and SEO pass.

Wireframe

Low fidelity first: a centred brand, a high-contrast nav, a one-line promise with two calls to action, and a signature image band.

User interface

A quiet ivory and charcoal system with a single rose accent, taken from the logo, on every call to action.

Clear service cards with honest “starting at” prices, and a dedicated bridal journey.
