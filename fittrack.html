FitTrack

A free, all-in-one fitness app I designed for myself: workouts, protein and weight tracking in one place, with AI suggestions on top.

Type
Personal project · mobile app
Timeline
Oct – Dec 2025
Role
Solo: research, UX & UI
Tools
Figma ↗

Design thinking

  1. 1Empathize

    Research showed people are motivated by visible results, but juggle separate apps and tedious manual entry.

  2. 2Define

    Three pain points: low motivation, fragmentation across apps, and generic plans with unclear metrics.

  3. 3Ideate

    One free app for workouts, protein and weight, with gentle AI suggestions the user can ignore.

  4. 4Prototype

    Gray-box wireframes, then digital wireframes and a mapped user flow, then high-fidelity screens in Figma.

  5. 5Test

    Next: usability testing of the AI suggestion flow and a full accessibility pass.

Me, the first user
Gym beginner · the brief

I wanted one app to track my training, free, without switching between three.

Goal: track the whole fitness journey, including daily protein and weight, with suggestions that feel earned.

Wireframe

Gray boxes first for every core screen: body-diagram picker, weekly plan, workout log, stats, protein, streaks and profile.

Once the layout held up, digital wireframes added the FIT.TRACK name and nav bar, still in grayscale so structure stayed the focus.

User interface

Gym energy: a black and maroon palette, high-contrast type and a bottom nav built for one hand mid-workout.

AI touches are labelled as suggestions, and the streak is a quiet flame and a day count, not a loud game.
