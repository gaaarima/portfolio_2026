About

Hi, I’m Garima. I design and build clear, human-centred digital products.

My path started outside design. Years in fine jewelry and restaurant management taught me to watch how people really work, and that is where every project still begins.

Today I turn messy everyday tasks into clear flows, then build the front end myself so the design ships as intended. I'm finishing a Bachelor's in Web & Digital Design (4.0 GPA) and hold the Google UX Design Certificate.
